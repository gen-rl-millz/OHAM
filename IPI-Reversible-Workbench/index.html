STASIS versus FLUX

A word followed by CC_F·CC_F has the same eight-state permutation as the original word, but a distinct operation transcript. Final-state identity does not imply identity of history.

Bennett closure

Three 3-bit registers: source X, clean workspace W, and clean destination Y. Work is uncomputed rather than overwritten.

The circuit contains 3 bitwise copy gates to initialize W, n programme gates, 3 bitwise copy gates to Y, n inverse gates, and 3 bitwise uncopy gates. That is 2n+9 elementary gates under this accounting—not a physical-energy estimate.
