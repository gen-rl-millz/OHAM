# Commercial licensing

OHAM is free and open to play with (see LICENSE). **Commercial use — a
product or service whose value derives substantially from OHAM —
requires a paid commercial license from the developer.**

- Developer and licensor: **Paul Phillips** — solo developer
- Contact: via **involvedinvolutions.com**
- The standing commercial term of the licensing model is a percentage of
  gross revenue on commercial use, with per-agreement specifics settled
  at signing. Terms are finalized with counsel per agreement.

If you are unsure whether your use is commercial, ask — validation,
research, benchmarking, and play are free by design.
